Timeline

The two charts below give the yearly counts of the two sources on the timeline. The first, 'Venetian printing', counts the documents granting a printing privilege among those edited by Fulin; the second, 'English inventions', counts the monopoly grants and patents in the lists of Hulme and Woodcroft. They count different things over different years, so they are drawn apart; their heights are not to be compared.

YearEvent
1469Venice: five-year printing privilege to Johannes de Spira (Fulin no. 1)
1474Venetian Senate patent act (parte of 19 March)
1486Venice: privilege to the author Sabellico (the earliest to an author among Fulin's documents, no. 3)
1517Venetian Senate revokes existing printing privileges and limits new ones to new books
1557Stationers' Company chartered; register entries begin (SRO no. 1, 1557-07-19)
1561England: Hulme's No. I (white soap)
1581Dutch Republic: the Buning data begin
1617Woodcroft's No. 1
1624Statute of Monopolies (21 Jac. I c. 3): patents for new inventions limited to 14 years or less
1637Entrance in the Stationers' Register becomes compulsory (SRO)
1710Statute of Anne; this site's printing data end in 1640 and cannot show the years around it
1852Woodcroft's list ends (1 October); it was compiled under the Patent Law Amendment Act, 15 & 16 Vict. c. 83, s. 32 (title page)

Years and events are taken from the sources used (Fulin, Hulme, Woodcroft, SRO) and the titles of the statutes. Statute citations: Acts of the English and, later, British Parliament passed before 1963 are cited by the regnal year of the session and the chapter number given to each Act passed in that session. 21 Jac. I c. 3 is chapter 3 of the session of the 21st year of James I (Jacobus), an Act of the English Parliament; 15 & 16 Vict. c. 83 is chapter 83 of the session spanning the 15th and 16th years of Victoria, an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom; s. 32 is section 32 of that Act.
