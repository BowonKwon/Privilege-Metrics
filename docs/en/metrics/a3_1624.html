Before and after the Statute of Monopolies (1624)

3.9 → 3.6grants per year, before → after
Key point

Mean grants per year were 3.9 in 1617–1623 and 3.6 in 1625–1631, with overlapping 95% intervals. Of the terms written in the entries, 17 of 24 in 1617–1623 are 21 years, and 77 of 81 in 1625–1640 are 14 years or less.

Definition
Mean grants per year in the seven years before and after 1624, the year of the Statute of Monopolies (21 Jac. I c. 3), and the terms written in the entries.
Formula
mean per year = grants in window ÷ 7; 95% interval = exact Poisson interval
How to read it
The yearly means of 1617–1623 and 1625–1631 are about the same. Across the same windows the term written in the entries moves from 21 to 14 years.
Caveats
A before-and-after comparison only; it does not say the Statute changed the number. The list before 1617 stops in 1603.
