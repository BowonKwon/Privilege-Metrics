P · Registering books

Shared, reassigned and cancelled entries

10.4%Reassigned, 1620s
Key point

Entries marked reassigned are 1.7% in the 1580s and 10.4% in the 1620s; shared entries are 13.6% in the 1630s.

Definition
Share of entries per decade that SRO marks 'shared' (entered by more than one person), 'reassigned' (made over to another) or 'cancelled'.
Formula
marked entries ÷ entries
How to read it
Entries marked reassigned grow from under 2% in the 1580s to 10% in the 1620s.
Caveats
The status is the SRO editors' judgment, used as given; an entry may carry several marks.
