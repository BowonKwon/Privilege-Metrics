Venetian printing privileges

240printing privileges 1492–1526
Key point

In Fulin's documents there are 240 printing privileges in 1492–1526, 73 (30%) of them to authors, editors or translators. By five-year period the most are in 1495–1499 (61) and the fewest in 1510–1514 (16).

Definition
Documents in Fulin's collection (1469–1526) that grant a printing privilege (an exclusive right to print, sell or use a type), per year and by type of beneficiary.
Formula
privilege documents per year, split author / printer
How to read it
By five-year period the most are in 1495–1499 and the fewest in 1510–1514; numbers rise again after 1515.
Caveats
Fulin collected the documents that survive; he notes that not every privilege granted left a record.
