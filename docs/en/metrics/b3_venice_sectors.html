Venice 1474–1550 by sector (secondary)

85 / 169Mills
Key point

Of the 169 Venetian patents of 1474–1550, mills account for 85 (50%), drainage and dredging 20, pumps 11 (Comino et al., Berveglieri 1995 sectors).

Definition
The 169 patents of Table A1 in Comino et al. (sectors of Berveglieri 1995), mapped to this site's field list.
Formula
published sector → this site's field (the mapping is ours)
How to read it
Mills make up about half, followed by drainage and dredging and by pumps.
Caveats
Counts are as published; only the mapping is ours.
