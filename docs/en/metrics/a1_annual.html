Invention grants per year, England

14,375Grants 1561–1852
Key point

England granted 14,375 invention privileges and patents in 1561–1852: 363 in the 83 years 1617–1699 and 11,955 in the 53 years 1800–1852. The peak year is 1845 (570).

Definition
New invention privileges and patents granted in the year: Hulme's list for 1561–1603; for 1617–1852 the numbered entries of Woodcroft's list less those granting an extension.
Formula
grants(year) = Hulme grants + Woodcroft entries − extension entries
How to read it
1604–1616 is blank because it falls between the two lists used here (Hulme ends in 1603, Woodcroft begins in 1617). Blank means unknown, not zero.
Caveats
Woodcroft numbers follow the date of the grant; the date used is the sealing date printed in the margin. Entries whose number the OCR lost are counted in the year of their neighbors. The two grants of 1853 are sealings of applications made under the law before 1852.
