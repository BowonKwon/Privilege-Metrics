Concentration of enterers

24%Top-five share, 1630s
Key point

The decade median of the top five enterers' yearly share is 30% in the 1560s and 24% in the 1630s.

Definition
Herfindahl index of enterer shares each year, and the share of the top five. An entry with several enterers is split among them.
Formula
HHI = Σ (enterer share)²; enterers keyed by the SRO name key
How to read it
Shows what share of each year's entries the top five stationers made.
Caveats
SRO name keys are used as given; a person recorded under two keys lowers the measured concentration.
