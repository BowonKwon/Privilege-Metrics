P · Registering books

Stationers' Register entries per year

10,453Entries 1557–1640
Key point

The register holds 10,453 entries for 1557–1640: 1,226 in the 1560s and 1,637 in the 1630s. No entries survive for July 1571 to July 1576.

Definition
Copy entries (permissions to publish) made in the register of the Stationers' Company each year.
Formula
entries per year by the SRO sort date
How to read it
About a thousand or more entries per decade: 1,226 in the 1560s, 1,637 in the 1630s.
Caveats
No entries survive for July 1571 to July 1576. Before 1637 entrance was not compulsory (SRO). Entries are not a count of books printed.
