P · Registering books

Entrance fees

92%6d share 1587–1640
Key point

Among entries with a stated fee, 6d is 17% in 1560–1581 and 92% in 1587–1640.

Definition
Share of entries with a stated fee whose fee is 6d, and the median fee, by year.
Formula
entries at 6d ÷ entries with a fee
How to read it
SRO notes that fees were reckoned by the number of sheets until 1582 and that 6d became standard from 1587; the change shows year by year.
Caveats
Entering several works at once gives a multiple of 6d.
