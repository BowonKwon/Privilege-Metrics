Jurisdictions compared by period

471 · 55Venice · England 1551–1600
Key point

The Venetian Senate granted 471 invention patents in 1551–1600 (secondary figure); England, from 1561, 55. Dutch documents issuing a privilege number 241 in 1581–1621, and Venetian printing-privilege documents 242 in 1469–1526. The counting units differ.

Definition
Privileges per half-century for England (our count), the Dutch Republic (our recount of the Buning data) and Venice (figures quoted from the literature), side by side.
Formula
sum per period, with the counting unit in its own column
How to read it
The units differ: Venetian inventions are Senate grants (secondary), Dutch figures are documents issuing a privilege, English figures are grants, Venetian printing figures are Fulin documents.
Caveats
The Venetian invention figures were not counted here, and the two published series (43, 126, 471 and 33, 116, 461) differ by 10 in each period. England 1551–1600 starts in 1561, and Hulme's 55 include four grants for regulating trade; 1601–1650 includes the 1604–1616 gap.

Prior results

  • Venetian Senate patents: 5 ad hoc privileges before 1474 (Mandich 1936), 43 in 1474–1500, 126 in 1501–1550, 471 in 1551–1600. — Comino, Galasso & Graziano (2017), NBER WP 24118, p. 7.
  • Kostylo (2010), citing Berveglieri 1995 (pp. 21–22), gives 33, 116 and 461 grants by the Senate alone for the same periods. Each is exactly 10 below the figures above; this site has not settled which is right. — Kostylo, J. (2010), 'From gunpowder to print', in Deazley, Kretschmer & Bently (eds.), Privilege and Property, Open Book Publishers, n. 5.
  • 2,130 patents granted by the Senate (Terra), 1474–1788. — Berveglieri, R. (2020), «Ingegnosi artificij», vol. 1, Cierre, preface.
  • 952 Dutch patent applications 1515–1797 (23 under Charles V and Philip II, 574 States General, 355 provincial States; secondary citation). — Doorman, G. (1940), Octrooien voor uitvindingen in de Nederlanden, as summarized in the review in Maandblad Oud-Utrecht, Sept. 1941.
