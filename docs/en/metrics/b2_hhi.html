B · Which trades

Field concentration

0.105Median from 1750
Key point

From 1750 the decade field concentration stays between 0.090 and 0.120; an even split across 17 fields gives 0.059.

Definition
Herfindahl index of field shares among classified grants in each decade, 'other' excluded.
Formula
HHI = Σ (field share)²
How to read it
Higher means grants concentrate in fewer fields. With 17 fields an even spread gives 1/17 ≈ 0.059.
Caveats
The early decades have few grants, so the index swings widely. The index inherits the precision of the field classification (75% weighted).

Names: Hulme and Woodcroft compiled lists of English monopoly grants and patents (1561–1603 and 1617–1852); Fulin edited documents on Venetian printing (1469–1526); Buning's data cover Dutch patents (1581–1621); SRO is Stationers' Register Online, a transcription of the register of the London Stationers' Company. References for the sources used on this page are under Sources below; those for all five are on the method page.

A figure such as 163/212 means that two values agree on 163 of the 212 entries checked. What is compared with what is in the row name: the rows on image checks, transcription, recoding and field agreement compare the site's value with the value another reader found or assigned from the page image or the text. But the row on dictionary r6 compares the field given by the previous dictionary (r6) with the coder; the row on the previous measurement compares r6 with the coders of its own sample (R2d); the row on agreement between coders compares the two coders with each other; 'only r7 right' and 'only r6 right' count, among the entries on which both coders gave the same answer, those where only one dictionary matched that answer, and 'both wrong' those where neither did. 95% CI (95% confidence interval, Wilson method) is a range for the true rate of agreement estimated from the sample: intervals made this way from repeated samples would contain the true value about 95 times in 100. Other things being similar, the smaller the sample, the wider it is. κ (Cohen's kappa) is agreement with the part expected by chance removed: 1 is complete agreement, 0 is no better than chance. The sample takes a set number per field (11 per field, 25 for 'other'), so its mix differs from that of the grants; the weighted precision therefore multiplies each field's agreement by that field's share of the grants ('other' included) and adds them up. The 95% CI on this card is an approximate interval for the unweighted share in the sample (for example 163/212), not a confidence interval for the weighted precision. Sample names (R2d, R2f, R2f-chg): sets of entries drawn for checking. On this card, R2d and R2f are stratified samples, the grants divided by field and drawn at random within each field. R2f-chg is a simple random sample from the grants whose field changed with the new dictionary. Dictionary names (r6, r7): versions of the word rules that assign fields (the field dictionary). The method page gives the history. Recoding is another person reading the same entry and assigning its categories again.
